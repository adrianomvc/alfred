# Phase: Inception (C.1) — "What?"

Goal: understand the demand before designing. Governed by D41 (stop on doubt) and the lane DoD (D25).

1. Detect stream/type (D5); if **Produto**, ingest external `inception-input` (D33) — do not redo business discovery.
2. **Intent analysis** (clarity/type/scope/complexity — D18).
3. Produce **`tech-inception`** (technical lens: affected systems/apps via reverse-eng, integrations, technical risks — D33).
4. Generate **requirements questions** in file (multiple choice + `[Answer]:`, D18) → **gate** → detect contradictions.
5. Pre-fill and **propose Risk Mode** (intent→checklist, D31); human confirms in Std/SAFE (D2.10).
6. Consolidate `problem`/`requirements`; record initial risks; **Inception DoD** → checkpoint.

Outputs: problem, tech-inception, requirements, risk (proposed), state updated.
