# Demand-type: Produto — operational prompt

Types: nova feature · melhoria funcional · nova jornada · UX · experimento · mudança de regra de negócio · evolução de produto.

## Path
- Two-lens Inception : the business lens usually ARRIVES READY from an external agent → ingest into `inception-input` (validate, do not redo). YOU produce the technical lens (`tech-inception`).
- Emphasis: Design/SDD (spec + acceptance) and Validation (user acceptance).
- Typical sub-activities: user-stories (journey/UX), application-design (new component).
- Mode: tends Standard; SAFE if end-customer / sensitive data / multi-app.

## Experiment shortcut
If it is an experiment with a clear hypothesis → FAST; capture learning in Operation (metrics + summary), not a heavy spec.

## Edge cases
- Business inception incomplete → ask the external source; do not invent business intent .
- Touches another app of the sigla → add it to `state` Apps tocadas; consider per-app risk override.

## Output example (pt-BR)
"Inception de negócio recebida e ingerida. Gerei a visão técnica (sistemas afetados, integrações, riscos). Propondo Standard."
