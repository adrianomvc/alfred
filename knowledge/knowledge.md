# Knowledge Registry

> Generated by `scripts/*/workflow/generate-registry.*` from each policy's frontmatter. Do not edit the table by hand.

Company knowledge is loaded by scope and event. This registry tells agents which policy file to load without reading the whole folder. Each policy declares its `scope`, retrieval `trigger`, and optional `macro` in frontmatter, mirroring how a host recalls knowledge on demand.

## Registry
| Policy | Scope | Trigger | Macro | Link |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `external-catalogs` | org | discovering a skill or documentation from an external catalog | `!catalogs` | [external-catalogs.md](external-catalogs.md) |
| `notification` | org | demand completion, checkpoint notification, escalation, incident stabilization, periodic status, or telemetry batch | `!notify` | [notification.md](notification.md) |
| `policy-template` | template | creating or changing a knowledge policy file | — | [policy-template.md](policy-template.md) |

## Rule
Load only the policy whose trigger matches the active event. Sigla/HUB and demand knowledge may harden these policies, but must not weaken company policy without an explicit human exception in the audit.
